# P07 stock allocation handoff

Stored-stock fulfillment extends P06 shipments. `0011_p07_stock_fulfillment.sql` is additive; planned 0007 is occupied. Migrations 0001–0010 are preserved. Existing external parcels receive no invented component allocation.

`shipmentCommands` confirms/revises/cancels/prepares/inspects through P03's checked-out UnitOfWork, command identity, immutable outcomes and audit. Confirmation uses `reserveOrder` instead of external intake receipt. Parcel custody has no receipt for already stored stock. Queue state is the common shipment's `awaiting_preparation` field. No monetary effect or remote outbox is created; P11 owns the actual canonical adapter.

`apps/api/src/modules/inventory/stock-fulfillment.ts` exports caller-owned `lockOrderPositions`, `reserveOrder`, `replaceOrder`, `releaseOrder`, `assertStockPreparation`, `unpackOrder` and `validateStockVariants`. They open no independent transaction. Acquire configuration and shipment aggregate locks before the sorted union of stock positions. Safe absent-position upsert/row locks and P05's current availability recheck protect independent clients. Commercial lines retain distinct declared unit values while requirements aggregate by variant. Missing stock or tariff rejects the entire transaction.

`readShipmentStock` returns immutable allocation links to actual reservations, their branch/revision/quantity/active/held state, actual shortage and bounded unpack history. Inventory variant history links the same claims and condition movements to numeric shipment references. Products and Parcels reference the same components; never add their totals.

Prepared contents remain sound and reserved until actual handover. Cancellation releases claims; prepared contents move sound→unavailable with `awaiting_unpack_inspection`. Changed prepared contents also retire old units to this state and require preparation of the newly allocated contents. A failed replacement restores the old revision/claims/conditions. Unprepared cancellation releases immediately. Inspection requires shipment `expectedVersion`, each pending identity and its exact `expectedRemaining`, actual sound/damaged/uncertain quantities and reason. Only inspected sound units become usable; damaged/uncertain remain unavailable. These are physical condition records without a fee, inbound receipt or financial posting.

P12 must lock the aggregate and positions, check current authority/version/source acceptance/preparation/custody and call `assertStockPreparation` before handover. Its shortage gate is effective even if the current reservation's cached hold is stale: it recomputes sound versus all active claims while positions are locked. P12 must implement actual sound-stock decrement, reservation closure and driver custody exactly once through P05 inventory effects. P07 does not implement handover.

P14 owns actual returns/source cycles. P21 owns general observation/condition adjustments; call P05 `StockPositionRepository.update/refreshHolds` under the position lock so every affected active claim is held together. Replenishment recomputes and releases holds; unrelated variants remain eligible. P07's unpack endpoint is not a general stock editor.
