## Formula and classification/date/branch contract

`shipping including packing − approved replacement waiver + whole storage period fee at start − employee salary/commission/additions + approved entitlement deductions − paid ordinary expenses − confirmed brand compensation + approved employee compensation share once`.

Ordinary expenses enter only when actually paid. Unpaid costs never entered in the system are not included. This limitation is displayed beside the formula and in exports. The report makes no claim of closed accounting periods.

EconomicEffect is closed in contracts: company/source/effect identity, category, signed integer-string amount, effectiveDate, recordedAt, historicalBranchId, batch, revision, ancestry and optional shipment/period/employee/incident. Report generation creates no new financial effect or second money journal. A salary's stable persisted snapshot identity is distinct from its policy source; it is not fabricated from salary payout cash.

Generic deposits/withdrawals, treasury transfer principal, opening balances/employee opening entitlement, employee advances/recovery, net salary cash payout and brand goods/principal/payout are excluded from additional profit. Unclassified P21 commercial/company-loss entries remain explicit unresolved sources, outside the result. Missing salary materialization, unresolved commission, absent/mismatched economic effects, duplicate identities and unknown historical branch are explicit exceptions. Duplicated identities are excluded in their entirety rather than arbitrarily choosing a copy. Unattributed proven facts remain in a separate company component; they are never spread over branches or exposed through partial branch scope. Unknown sources never erase unrelated valid facts.

## Queries, snapshot and reconciliation

The adapter reads real PostgreSQL native sources in one REPEATABLE READ view with P23 rows/context/totals/coverage/digests/asOf. Company total equals branch components plus an explicit unattributed component when present. Filters use parameters and deterministic effective/recorded dates plus effect identity ordering. No receipt-component, allocation or correction-history fan-out is used to derive an earning. Classification IDs are established independently of the selected date window, so a later recorded correction to an older work period is not incorrectly classified as unknown in the newer recorded window.

Migration0028 adds only correction-parent, payroll work/branch, frozen salary and frozen category-detail indexes. It copies no old cash into revenue. Populated0027 upgrade/idempotence and unchanged source/business hashes are tested in [migration.json](migration.json). Real `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE,BUFFERS,FORMAT JSON)` and isolated source timings are in profit-database.json under query-behavior. Tiny fixtures may correctly use sequential scans; this is functional index/query evidence, not a production capacity promise.

Findings freeze target/source links, observed versions, exact economic delta where known, branch scope and asOf. Source checkpoint findings use received/applied sequence evidence and a null money delta; event counts are not amounts. Read-only checks compare account cache to the money journal, transfer state to transit history, wallet remaining lots/allocations/debt to its journal, storage receipt/allocation/refund credit to its journal, and economic domain records to their effects. Findings open existing domain/recovery/settlement review paths and post no adjustment. A refresh creates new evidence; old snapshots retain the original finding.

The only new maintenance primitive is finance/accounts/rebuild.ts. An operator already authorized for accounts **and** settlements supplies a reviewed reason and observed journal/cache totals, takes the same account writer lock, and updates only the rebuildable projection with audit. Stale observations, a negative source result and unauthorized/shared-scope access fail closed. It is deliberately absent from report HTTP and UI. A07 invokes it through an authorized UnitOfWork in an isolated database. For other immutable-source faults, tests first prove normal writes are rejected, disable only the named immutable guard inside a test savepoint, observe the exact discrepancy, then roll back the fault/guard. They do not invent cash repair.
