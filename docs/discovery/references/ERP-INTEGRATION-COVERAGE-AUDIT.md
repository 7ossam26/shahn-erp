## Recovery obligations and unfinished evidence

1. **Source commit and retry identity.** Native ERP authorization, intended state/revision, native actor audit and exact immutable public envelope commit together. HTTP runs outside the database transaction under a durable fenced lease. Timeout, lost response, malformed body or later `401/403` can leave acceptance unknown. Preserve the same action/envelope and use the family-supported recovery path. A retained rejection requires reviewed new intent, not new IDs until success. Complete action-result/compaction and feature recovery reads remain part of the pending foundation audit.
2. **Signed receipt.** Verify raw UTF-8 bytes, tenant/recipient/key scope, unambiguous headers, HMAC and timestamp window; then closed payload/version/identity validation. Commit scoped ID/body/sequence before returning the exact receipt. Duplicate identical delivery is safe; changed body or sequence collision is a conflict. This audit read the complete published signature vector but did not calculate or test it. The test-only public key is not a deployment secret.
3. **Atomic application.** Commit projection changes, transition history and processed marker together. Preserve `receivedThrough`, `receivedHigh`, `appliedThrough`, `projectedThrough`, `snapshotThrough` and `historyComplete`. Rollback/restart/concurrent-worker verification requires a real database, not mocks. No cross-system ACID claim follows from local transactions.
4. **Ordering and gaps.** Per-recipient aggregate sequence is not global order. Persist known streams and cross-stream dependencies. Buffer gaps and use bounded replay. No general account-wide stream discovery endpoint is promised. A quiet queue does not prove no unseen correction exists.
5. **History loss and snapshots.** `410` replay failure and bounded `503` snapshot unavailability have visible recovery states. A newer authoritative snapshot can recover current state atomically without inventing events, historic receipts, visits or cash. Late mandatory transitions below snapshot coverage still need once-only historical handling without recounting already-covered stock. Captured current-state and report examples remain partly unread.
6. **Custody races.** Actual receipt, disposition, retry, correction and redispatch can invalidate each other under current revisions. Keep original cycle history and actual physical balance. ERP transfer/customer dispatch cannot allocate the same goods simultaneously. No generic manual adjustment may bypass Tawsel execution or receipt compatibility.
7. **Money.** Reported collection, physical full remittance, eligible brand balance and actual brand payout are distinct. Approved queue policy holds the affected amount for a known sync gap and sends a later posted-money difference for authorized linked review. It does not freeze every brand or rewrite posted/paid history. Session 019's per-visit tariff rule does not itself establish recipient shipping behavior in Tawsel.
8. **Operational limits and restart.** Define callback deployment, secret acceptance/rotation, source/service credential recovery, payload limit, worker bounds, audit visibility and coherent restore of business data plus all command/event identities. The supplied sender permits 2 MiB while the reference receiver permits 1 MiB; choose and test an effective ERP limit before claiming support. No retry-count purge or automatic history deletion is selected.
