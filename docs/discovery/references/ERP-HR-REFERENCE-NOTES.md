## Current owner choices and open differences

- Simple HR covers all employees; Tawsel driver onboarding is not an HR prerequisite (ERP-D-072).
- Salary and commission are independently enabled sections. Commission chooses a percentage of base shipping or a fixed amount for each actual eligible visit, attributed to its performing driver, including repeated visits by different drivers. It is calculated automatically from ERP-recorded work and earned independently of brand payment. Exclude packing and show salary/commission/total separately (ERP-D-078, ERP-D-085, ERP-D-086).
- Salary-payment treasury selection does not itself approve an ERP treasury module, POS shift gates, manager-specific permissions, or the reference's payment locks. Use the owner's modular access model.
- Separate staff/person records, login accounts and operational driver references as concepts. Automatic commission now requires stable ERP attribution for the relevant driver employee; do not force every employee into Tawsel. No Tawsel HR module is requested.
- Manual bonuses/overtime, work days/hours/weekly day off, net-zero/deficit carry and paid/past-period protection remain approved. Session 009 explicitly removes partial salary payout (ERP-D-087) and replaces the separate-manual-repayment-only rule with recorded advances reducing the applicable payroll net (ERP-D-088). Salary 6,000 less advance 1,000 means final salary payment 5,000. Partial brand payout remains allowed.
- Advance cash issuance, its payroll recovery allocation and final net salary payment are distinct. Do not create a fictitious cash repayment or recover the same advance twice. The owner's floor-at-zero and carry policy differs from the reference's negative-net rejection and must be preserved. Current unpaid/future adjustments must preserve original paid/past calculations. Schedule fields and manual overtime do not approve attendance-derived pay, leave, tax or a full HR suite.
- ERP-D-191 / ERP-R-200 close ERP-Q-166: for an employee who starts or leaves partway through a month, retain the configured salary. The responsible person calculates the required reduction outside ERP, records an ordinary deduction and pays the resulting net through the normal payroll action. The owner rejects both the proposed special first/last-month salary override and automatic day-based proration. Preserve the deduction's reason, actor and period, normal current-unpaid-period authority, paid/past-period locks, net-zero/carry behavior and single net payout. This does not revoke the separately approved ability to change current unpaid or future salary settings for other legitimate reasons.
- ERP-D-190 / ERP-R-199 assign complete visit shipping revenue to the branch dispatching the customer-delivery work; employee cost attribution remains ERP-D-131. ERP-D-194 / ERP-R-203 set a base-50-plus-packing-5 company tariff to 55 for each actual eligible visit, while the percentage commission remains based on 50. These choices do not add transfer commission: internal goods transport remains covered by salary under ERP-D-180/183.

## Review limits

Keep a simple balance, positive/negative movements, payout action and complete period history.
