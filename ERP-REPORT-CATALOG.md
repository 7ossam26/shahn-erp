## Shared design constraints

- Session024 controls the current financial formula: storage revenue is the entire fixed fee in its service-start month; partial/advance receipts are cash/credit facts, not another revenue source. Entitlement deductions reduce employee cost, advance recovery does not, and incident recovery counts once. An explicit company-funded replacement-shipping waiver produces zero net shipping revenue with normal commission and preserves actual goods due. These rules qualify the historical full-tariff/gross-cost wording below; see ERP-D-201..204 and domain sections10–14.

- Filters must fit the report: period and date meaning, authorized branch, brand, driver/employee, status, service, geography/area, reason, payment method and reference where relevant. Omit irrelevant filters. Preserve the user's selected scope when navigating to detail.
- Enforce company, branch and module authorization on queries, totals and exports. A filter is not an authorization boundary. ERP-D-160/161 explicitly allow a payout-screen user to see and pay the shared company-level eligible balance for accessible brands, with branch breakdown; this does not grant unrestricted company-wide report or account access. ERP-D-187 separately grants tracking-screen users company-wide operational shipment search and full journey detail. That read exception does not expand report/export scope, financial visibility or write permissions.
- Show which business date a period uses: actual visit, accepted outcome, remittance, payout, storage service period or payroll period. Event receipt time must not silently replace the underlying business date. ERP-D-164 selects calendar-month payroll and midnight-to-midnight daily reporting in Africa/Cairo, with UTC timestamps retained. A round may cross midnight without being split; storage retains its separate anniversary periods. Exact allocation of particular source movements to those periods remains design work.
- Distinguish shipments from attempts/visits, gross recipient money from company revenue, reported money from actual remittance, eligible brand balance from payout, and earned commission from salary payout.
- ERP-D-190 / ERP-R-199 attribute each actual customer-delivery visit's complete shipping revenue, including the agreed packing uplift, to the branch dispatching that work. ERP-D-194 / ERP-R-203 charge the full tariff on each actual eligible visit: base shipping 50 plus packing uplift 5 means company revenue of 55 per visit, with a percentage-commission base of 50. An internal transfer is not a customer visit and creates no additional revenue. Preserve each visit's original branch, tariff and identity; recipient money remains a distinct reported fact whose repeated-payment mapping is covered by TAWSEL-CHECK-002.
- Distinguish physical on-hand, reserved, available, held, lost/damaged and actually received return quantities. Offered return is not stock receipt or availability.
- ERP-D-175 through ERP-D-189 settle ERP-only inter-branch transport, grouped contents, reservation/eligibility, assigned-branch sender/receiver authority, salary-covered driver transport, actual discrepancies and custody-safe cancellation. There is no extra brand charge. Active company drivers may carry a transfer during a delivery round, with source-branch and known idle/in-branch states prioritized and ongoing-round status visible. Source handover and destination receipt are separate confirmed events; in-transit quantities are unavailable at either branch. A previously attempted shipment may transfer after actual branch receipt, with later destination dispatch subject to contract verification. Inspect sealed parcels by identity/exterior, count loose stock and use one manifest per physical trip.
- ERP-D-177 / ERP-R-186 require searchable shipment whereabouts/custodian, current state and history, using the owner's timeline reference. ERP-D-187 / ERP-R-196 close ERP-Q-162: users with tracking-screen access may search all company shipments and see their full operational journey. This is an operational read exception rather than a new standalone report or broader financial/export permission. The reference does not introduce GPS, percentage progress or ETA data.
- Show pending reconciliation, missing evidence and data freshness where material. ERP-D-174 accepts idempotent automatic retry, a hold on affected payout eligibility for known synchronization gaps, and an authorized Settlements review queue for accepted corrections conflicting with posted money. Preserve original movements; neither replay nor an empty queue proves a cash movement or the absence of unseen events.
- ERP-D-106 selects on-screen, Excel and print/PDF output for the selected reports. Require clear Arabic/RTL layout, readable labels, period/filter context, consistent amounts/totals, suitable number/date formats and print pagination. These are future acceptance checks, not verified rendering. Excel intake remains deferred. No external BI service or paid reporting product is selected.
- Selecting a report with an unresolved data dependency requires defining that workflow and its acceptance criteria; do not populate it using guessed formulas.
- ERP-D-168 keeps business and audit history without automatic deletion in V1. Deactivate referenced master records and preserve their historical identity/display. Backup retention does not define business-record deletion.
