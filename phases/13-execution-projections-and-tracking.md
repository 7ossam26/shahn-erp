## 4. Execution and money rules

P22 measures representative p50/p95, sample count, load and network bounds. A missed target remains recorded engineering work and must not be hidden by moving acknowledgement earlier or weakening durable processing.

The unique visit key is company/source plus `taskId`, `dispatchCycleId`, `attemptId`. Accepted `current.arrivalRecorded` or equivalent embedded accepted arrival/history can establish it. Heading, a retry admission, a branch arrival, route publication or `no-answer` alone cannot. `outcomes.Record.arrival` is nullable. Where arrival evidence is delayed, label earning awaiting visit evidence and preserve the pending financial basis. One attempt correction cannot create another visit. A distinct retry attempt earns only after its own actual arrival; it can belong to another driver.

Arrival before outcome establishes the visit and its captured fee/commission basis; a missing outcome is not evidence that the recipient refused payment. Keep payer allocation visibly pending until the effective accepted outcome and retained native payer policy determine it. Do not post an ordinary unpaid-brand debit merely because the outcome event is late. Delivered-goods credit likewise requires the accepted effective delivered/report basis. Once that evidence arrives, complete the allocation through the same unique visit/outcome identities without a second earning.

Keep Tawsel `Record.collection.reported`, `goods`, `shipping`, `unpaidShipping`, `shippingStatus` unchanged. Null reported money differs from zero. Current company no-answer may have `reported:null`, shipping 0, unpaidShipping 0 and `shippingStatus:"not-attempted"`. If separate accepted arrival proves an eligible visit, ERP records its native brand shipping debit/company earned fee without rewriting that canonical report. This is IP-GAP-003 / CHECK-001 acceptance, supported by existing fields but requiring a real human arrival→no-answer demonstration.

For normal policy, full tariff = captured base shipping + fixed packing uplift on every actual eligible customer visit. Base 50 + uplift 5 means 55 for a refused visit and 55 for a later successful visit. Recipient-paid shipping satisfies that visit's fee; do not also debit the brand by 55. An eligible unpaid visit creates the selected brand liability, even if unexpected debt results; an allow-negative preference cannot erase actual execution. Consume an existing shipping-cover reservation into that fee exactly once. Internal ERP transfers, branch service stops and unvisited postponements have no visit revenue/commission.

Goods 100 + goods 150 and shipping 50 delivered/reported means pending brand goods credit 250 and company earned shipping 50; the 250 is not eligible for payout yet. Create the pending credit lot and typed journal source immediately through P03's existing wallet kernel. P16 later releases eligibility upon actual full remittance. No inbox or projection handler writes a company account receipt, pays a brand or executes payroll. Goods prepaid to the brand create no second goods credit.

For D203 approved incident-linked company-funded replacement, retain standard tariff 50 and waiver 50, recipient shipping 0, brand shipping 0, net shipping revenue 0. Actual goods due 250 remains goods report 250 and pending goods credit 250 after eligible delivery. A distinct prepaid-goods fact is required to make goods zero. Preserve the normal commission despite the waiver. Do not turn the waiver into an expense cash movement, waive all COD, or add an employee-funded replacement choice.

Commission uses the visit's explicit effective driver-to-employee association and captured pay agreement. Percentage commission applies to base shipping only, never packing uplift; fixed commission applies per eligible visit. Represent percentage as integer basis points, round each visit half up to the nearest piastre, then aggregate. It does not wait for brand payment. Persist employee, agreement version, rate, commission basis, work branch and source visit on the earning. Missing/ambiguous links create visible unresolved earning basis, not zero commission or name-based attribution. Later relinking/rate changes are prospective.
