## 3. Recovery services and exact wire boundary

Implement `integration.getDeliveryStatus` GET `/api/v1/integration/deliveries` with cursor and limit 1–100 and `integration.getDeliveryDetail` GET `/api/v1/integration/deliveries/{eventId}` with documented attempt pagination. These show pending/sending/received/failed delivery and attempts, not application success. Sender `projectionStatus` remains unknown. A signed event acknowledged `received` means only durable ERP receipt; ERP's own committed facts/checkpoints determine applied state.

Use `integration.retryDelivery` POST `/api/v1/integration/commands/integration.retryDelivery`, concrete `RetryDeliveryCommand`, `integration.manage`. It asks for delivery retry of the same committed event identity, not re-execution of the originating business action or forced projection. Likewise, unknown source commands retain their original immutable `actionId` and use their allowed retained result endpoint or exact replay. Never replace an unknown command with a fresh business ID. Classify definite rejection, pending 202, retryable outage, authentication/configuration block and semantic conflict separately.

For a known stream call `integration.replayEvents` GET `/api/v1/integration/replay` with exact `aggregateType` (`task`, `assignment`, `trip`, `workday`, `return-request`, `integration`), UUID `aggregateId`, `afterSequence` starting from the required gap and bounded limit 1–100. Follow `nextAfterSequence`; validate every returned event against its concrete sender variant and source identity before durable receipt. Replay and live callbacks feed the same inbox/semantic deduplication, not two posting paths. Stream registry comes from known accepted commands, scoped task listings and received/replayed events. No undocumented global stream-discovery API is assumed.

A 410 `replay_expired` means required history is unavailable; it is not a blanket policy to purge everything after 30 days. A 503 or transport timeout does not mean an empty complete stream. Persist the exact requested range, failure and last safe checkpoint. After a bounded retry classify unresolved reconstruction as support work; do not spin without limit or rewrite timestamps to pretend the queue is current.

Use `integration.getReconciliationSnapshot` GET `/api/v1/integration/reconciliation` for the scoped aggregate. Validate the closed consumer `Snapshot`, identity, versions, coverage sequence and `history:"current-state-only"`. It can restore current state through its reported coverage, but cannot supply invented missing events, individual visits, historical money or physical receipts. An oversized/unavailable bounded reconstruction may return 503. Retain old confirmed state and explicit incomplete recovery instead of accepting a partial reconstruction as complete.

Report actual committed application counters with `integration.reportAppliedCheckpoint` POST `/api/v1/integration/commands/integration.reportAppliedCheckpoint`, concrete consumer `ReportCommand`, and inspect GET `/api/v1/integration/applied-checkpoint` (`integration.getAppliedCheckpoint`). Use the same aggregate query identity. Persist report intent through P11's outbox; do not advance local counters because the report itself was delivered. Reports remain `receiver-reported`, not a third-party audit of ERP accounting.

All these operations require the actual configuration allowlist and `integration.manage`. Bootstrap operator, service and human authority remain separate. `consumer.receiveSignedEvent`, `consumer.getStatus` and `source.getCommandStatus` are on the external ERP/reference host; they are not extra Tawsel endpoints. The connector cannot call human current activity, outcomes, correction, round start/end, branch service, device takeover or offline synchronization to repair missing evidence.
