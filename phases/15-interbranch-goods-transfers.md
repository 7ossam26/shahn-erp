## 3. Business rules and authority

One manifest has one company, source branch, different destination branch, active company operational driver and planned physical trip. Source choices are the current user's assigned branches; destination may be any branch in the same company. Creating/sending and receiving are separately granted screens. Receipt requires assigned destination scope. A multi-branch user can act at both ends when granted both screens; there is no mandatory separate person and no title-based bypass. Recheck permissions and assignment at command execution, including cached screens and result retrieval.

Allow active company drivers from any branch, including those on a Tawsel customer round. Rank evidence-supported at-branch/no-active-round candidates first; among comparable availability prefer source-branch affiliation. Show current round and evidence age or unknown status before selection. Affiliation is not physical presence. Source monitoring is received-evidence-only, provides no GPS/contact guarantee, and is not a reservation lock. Recheck that the native driver is still active at handover; do not attempt to lock/change Tawsel's route or add a stop.

Whole prepared shipments move intact with their existing order/component allocation. They cannot simultaneously go to a customer, another transfer or brand handover. An external brand parcel remains one parcel identity and does not create loose SKU units. Loose product/variant units must be sound, physically at source and unreserved. Mixed brands are allowed; preserve every line's owning brand and product/variant. Source and destination are single values for a manifest; additional planned trips use additional manifests. Unexpected short receipt remains a discrepancy on the original trip.

Before actual handover, cancellation releases the manifest's reservations and leaves physical source quantities unchanged. Actual handover subtracts source physical custody and creates driver/manifest custody atomically; neither branch can use the in-transit units. Destination confirmation adds **only actual received** quantities to destination, subtracting those quantities from transit. Sound loose units become available, damaged/uncertain units remain unavailable, and prepared parcel components remain allocated to their shipment. Missing quantities stay unresolved with carrier/incident evidence; they do not automatically become lost or source stock.

After handover, a Cancel click cannot restore source quantities. A genuine physical return to source uses a separately recorded actual receipt against the manifest's remaining custody and the same condition controls. Preserve the original intended destination and full trail; do not edit the manifest into an invented reverse trip. Destination receipt and source return compete for the same remaining quantity. An incident investigation may resolve a remaining discrepancy later through P18/P21; this phase must supply a typed candidate/link without automatically confirming liability.

For sealed whole parcels inspect parcel identity and exterior. Do not demand routine opening or certify unseen inner contents. Record suspected internal damage/shortage as an observation for incident review. Loose goods are counted by whole quantity. A user must be able to distinguish received sound, received damaged and not received; an “accept all” default cannot imply an unperformed inspection.

## 4. Persistence, calculations and transactions

Extend existing inventory with additive `goods_transfer`, `goods_transfer_line`, immutable handover and receipt facts, receipt lines/condition, remaining carrier custody and preparation reservations. Maintain one stable manifest UUID and a digits-only human reference generated by a database sequence; gaps are acceptable. Keep separate native `commandId` for create/reserve, handover, cancellation and each actual receipt, with canonical payload digest and expected manifest version. Add company-scoped foreign keys, unique source-command facts and indexes for source/destination/state/carrier/date/search. Backfill nothing as received merely because an old status is complete; any legacy unknown custody remains explicitly unresolved.

For each branch/brand/variant: physical = sound + unavailable; reserved = order reservations + loose-transfer reservations; available = max(sound − reserved,0); shortage = max(reserved − sound,0). Reservation is a subset of physical stock, not another quantity added to it. A packed stored-stock order and its parcel view describe the same allocated contents. On whole-parcel transfer, move the components and their order allocation exactly once. On loose transfer, consume its reservation when physical custody leaves. An actual correction can expose reserved greater than sound; preserve physical truth and block affected unhanded work rather than hiding shortage.

All lines of each native action are atomic. Use P03's one-client `UnitOfWork`, access checks and immutable audit. Lock the manifest/affected shipments, then sorted stock positions `(branch,brand,variant)` and reservations in the shared global order. Precreate/upsert missing destination rows safely before locking them. Validate all lines before posting any custody/stock effect. If the final line is unavailable, no earlier line transfers and no misleading successful history is written. Do not put an HTTP read inside the stock transaction.

Receipt locks the remaining manifest quantities and both relevant current custody records. For each line require nonnegative whole received quantities, at least one actual receipt, and received sound + damaged no greater than unresolved balance. For a whole parcel do not split its parcel identity into multiple simultaneous custodians. Store receipt actor/time and observed condition; derive remaining quantity rather than trusting a client-supplied total. Simultaneous destination receipt and source-return receipt must yield one valid winner for overlapping units. Repeated identical `commandId` returns the recorded result; changed body conflicts. Authorization is rechecked before exposing retained results.

The transfer posts no brand-wallet debit/credit, shipping revenue, visit fact or ordinary commission. Prevent a generic “shipment delivered” event from making those entries. Preserve work-branch attribution: an eventual customer visit uses that customer-dispatch branch, not automatically original intake or transport source. Storage attribution remains its own agreement. P13's timeline appends native transfer facts with truthful source, driver and actual receipt times.
